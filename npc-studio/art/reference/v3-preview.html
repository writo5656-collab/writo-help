NPC Studio V3 preview

How the new menus, camera, outline, fog and green screen will look and behave. Tap through it like in game. It's a drawing of the add-on, not Minecraft itself, so blocks and lighting are simplified. On a phone, turn it sideways.

  • Display → tap a skin head
  • Size › → drag the slider
  • Trim Studio → change pattern/material
  • Pose → Gizmo, drag on the world
  • World → Depth Fog
  • Camera → Peek
  • Layers → Green Screen, Photo Mode

Side panel (left)

The new main menu: tabs, head grids, one-tap switches. Built on the menu system Minecraft uses for add-on menus, with our own design file.

Live editor (grey box)

Mojang's new live menu. Sliders and dropdowns change the NPC while you drag; it doesn't close. Its look is Mojang's and can't be changed, and where it sits on screen is only certain once it runs in game.

Cyan outline

Marks the selected NPC. Every player in the world sees it. Hidden automatically in Peek and Photo Mode.

Pyramid cameras

Green = selected, blue = others. The frame gets wider or narrower with the camera's FOV.
