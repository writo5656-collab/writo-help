NPC Studio panel – phone mockup

Tap through it like in-game. Try: Pose tab → Rotate → drag on the world → Done → Yes. Or Camera tab → Fly Cam. Turn on the tap map to see which parts are forms (orange) and which are live (green).

Tap anything in the panel.
Tabs follow your real menus. Display = Skin & Size, Wardrobe, Animation. Pose = Pose Editor. Presets = presets, saved poses, bulk tools. World and Camera are the same as now.
Gizmo flow. Rotate or Move closes the panel and starts your Pose Mode: hotbar tools, corner readout, yellow frame on the part. Done or sneak asks Keep this pose? Yes keeps it, No undoes it, and the panel returns on Pose.
In this demo the drag is flat (2D). In the addon it is your look direction in 3D. The confirm step before the panel returns is new; today Done just ends the mode.
