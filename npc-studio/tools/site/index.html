After downloading
  1. If the file saved as .zip, rename it to .mcpack.
  2. Open it: Minecraft imports the pack.
  3. World Settings → Resource Packs: activate it and move it above NPC Studio.
  4. In game: Spawn NPC → Custom skin slots. Your skins show with their faces and names.

Questions

Do I need NPC Studio V2 installed?

Yes. This page only makes a skin pack; the NPCs come from the NPC Studio V2 add-on on CurseForge.

My download is a .zip, not .mcpack

Rename the file so it ends in .mcpack, then open it. Minecraft imports it.

My skins don't show in game

In your world's Resource Packs, make sure the skin pack is active and sits above NPC Studio in the list.

Are my skins uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device; the pack is built right in your browser.

Can I add more skins later?

Yes. Build a new pack with all your skins and replace the old one. NPCs keep their slot numbers.
