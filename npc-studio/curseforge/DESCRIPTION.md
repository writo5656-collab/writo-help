# 🎬 NPC Studio V3

**Build Minecraft thumbnails and cinematic shots entirely in-game.**
Spawn NPCs, pose them, dress them, put them on horses and film it all. No commands, no outside tools, everything is in clean in-game menus.

By **NoxeelMC** · 📺 [youtube.com/@NoxeelMC](https://youtube.com/@NoxeelMC)

## 🆕 New in V3
- 🗂️ **Studio Panel**: one dark panel on the left with six tabs (Display, Pose, Presets, World, Camera, Layers)
- 🎛️ **Live editors**: drag a slider and the NPC changes while the menu stays open
- 🔷 **Cyan outline** on the selected NPC · 📐 **pyramid cameras** that show the real shot size
- 🖥️ **Camera Monitor** to peek through cameras · 📸 **Photo Mode** for clean screenshots
- 🟩 **Green screen + Layer Shot**: NPC on green and background without NPCs, from the same camera
- 🌫️ **Depth Fog** · 🧱 **block, item & particle props** · 👥 **crowds** · 🎬 **scene presets** · 🧣 **capes**

## ✨ What's inside

### 🧍 NPCs
- **12 themed skins + 20 custom skin slots** for your own skins, with classic or slim (Alex) arms
- **Rename, move, clone, lock** and **save/load presets** (skin + pose + gear together)
- **Bulk tools:** spawn up to 50 NPCs at once or apply a preset to every NPC nearby, so you can build a whole crowd or army in seconds
- **Size 10% – 500%**

### 🎯 Posing
- **Pose Mode:** look at a body part, then rotate or move it with the gizmo on your hotbar
- **45+ ready poses:** idle, running, fighting, sitting, lying down, flying, kneeling, waving, emotes, riding…
- Precise sliders, mirror left/right, copy/paste poses, undo/redo, your own pose library

### 🛡️ Armor & items
- **Armor trims + enchant glint** with the Trim Studio fitting room: see every pattern and colour on the NPC as you pick it
- **Copy My Outfit:** the NPC gets your exact armor, with enchants, trims, names and dye
- **Search Item:** type a name like "totem" or "diamond sword", and items from other add-ons work too

### 🎞️ Animation
- **19 looping animations:** breathing, walking, running, waving, talking, dancing, sword swing…
- **Keyframe Animator** and **Record My Movement** (walk a path, the NPC replays it)
- **Look At Players:** the NPC's head follows you

### 🎥 Camera
- **Fly Cam:** fly the camera with your joystick, tap the screen to lock the shot
- **30 cinematic shots:** orbit, dolly zoom, crane, drone fly-by, bird's eye, over-the-shoulder, duel shots…
- Named cameras with FOV, smooth paths, aim at / follow an NPC and camera shake
- **Clean shots:** your body, held items, name tags and the HUD disappear while filming

### 🐴 Mobs
- **Beast Whistle:** spawn vanilla mobs as props and freeze them in place
- **NPCs ride mobs:** horses, camels, wolves and more
- **Mob poses:** horse rearing, wolves/cats sitting, fox sleeping, warden roar…

### 🌤️ World
Quick time-of-day and weather controls from the menu.

### 📖 Handbook
An in-game book that explains every feature, handed to every new player.

## 🚀 Getting started
1. Import the `.mcaddon` and activate **NPC Studio V3** on your world (both packs)
2. Join the world. You get the **Director's Baton**, **Cine Camera**, **Camera Monitor**, **Beast Whistle** and the **Handbook**
3. Tap the air with the Baton to open the **Studio Panel**, or tap an NPC to edit it

**Commands:** `/npcstudio:npc` · `/npcstudio:exitcam` · `/npcstudio:lockcam` · `/npcstudio:cameras` · `/npcstudio:handbook`

## ⚙️ Requirements
- **Minecraft Bedrock 26.30 or newer**
- ✅ **No Beta APIs / experiments needed anymore**
