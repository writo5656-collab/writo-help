Look at a body part and rotate or move it with the red, green and blue gizmo. Walk around while you pose, so you can check it from every side. 45+ ready poses and 19 looping animations, with undo, redo and mirror.

New: spawn a zombie, warden, horse, creeper, wolf, spider and 23 more Minecraft mobs, with their real models and textures, and pose them part by part like an NPC. Legs, wings, head and body all move with the same gizmo, and presets and animations work on them too. Turn any NPC into a mob in Display → Mob.

The Studio panel sits on the left with six tabs: Display, Pose, Presets, World, Camera, Layers. The Live Editor is one screen that never reloads: every toggle, slider and list changes the NPC the moment you touch it. Come To Me and Look Where I Look sit right at the top.

  • Display: skin, mob, 3D model, cloak, nametag, sneak, living, animation, rotation X/Y/Z, scale, look at me, flip, on fire, bright
  • Inventory: six slots, trims and enchants, Item State for bows and crossbows, and Hold poses for any item, even guns from other add-ons
  • World: time, weather, gamerules, world bright, depth fog
  • Layers: hide NPCs, cameras, outlines or name tags for clean shots

8 studio capes, 43 Minecraft capes and 7 slots for your own. Pick them from pictures in Display → Cloak.

Open the free Pack Builder on your phone or PC. Get a player's skin by name, add capes and Blockbench 3D models, preview them in 3D and download one small pack. Nothing is uploaded: the pack is built in your browser.

Save an NPC as a preset and send it as a code (Presets → Share Code). Your friend taps Import Code and pastes it. Or turn codes into a preset pack file on the website, and PC players can share exact copies as .mcstructure files.

Place cameras, fly a camera with the joystick, preview any camera in one tap and run 30 cinematic shots. Thirds and cross guides, green screen backdrops and Photo Mode.

Made in NPC Studio

Real screenshots from the game, no editing.

  1. Download the .mcaddon file and open it. Minecraft imports all packs.
  2. Create a world (or edit one) and turn on NPC Studio in Behavior Packs. The resource pack comes with it.
  3. In game, use the Director's Baton from your hotbar: tap an NPC to edit it, or open the panel to spawn one.

Updating? Delete the old NPC Studio packs first, then make a new world. The version number shows at the top of the panel.

Questions

Do I need experiments? No. NPC Studio uses only the stable script API.

Does it work on phone? Yes. Everything is built for touch first, and tested on Android.

Can I pose mobs? Yes: 29 Minecraft mobs, part by part. Other mobs can still be spawned as frozen props and ridden.

Can NPCs hold items from other add-ons? Yes. Give the item, then pick a Hold pose so it is held up or aimed instead of hanging at the side.

Can I use my own skins? Yes: 20 custom skin slots, 7 cape slots and 10 3D model slots, filled with the Pack Builder website.

Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us in the comments or on Discord. Your feedback decides what comes next.
