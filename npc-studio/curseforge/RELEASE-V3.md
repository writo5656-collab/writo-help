# NPC Studio V3 — file notes for CurseForge

**Display name:** NPC Studio V3
**File:** `NPC Studio V3.mcaddon` (NPC Studio BP + RP only, internal version 4.27.0)
**Release type:** Release · **Game versions:** 26.30, 26.40, 26.50, 26.60

## Changelog (paste into the file's changelog box)

**NPC Studio V3: the studio update**

- New Studio panel: Display, Pose, Presets, World, Camera and Layers tabs on the left, your NPC on the right
- Live Editor: one screen that stays open, every change shows at once
- Pose Mode with a red / green / blue gizmo; walk around while you pose; 45+ poses and 19 animations
- 58 capes: 8 studio capes, 43 Minecraft capes and 7 slots for your own
- Custom skins, capes and 3D models with the free Pack Builder website
- Posable mobs: 29 Minecraft mobs (zombie, creeper, warden, horse, wolf, spider...) with their real look, posed part by part like an NPC
- Items from other add-ons: a Hold pose for anything in the hand (guns aim with one or two hands)
- Share presets as codes (Share Code / Import Code) or as preset pack files
- Cameras with a real shot frame, Fly Cam, 30 cinematic shots, Thirds and Cross guides, Photo Mode, green screen
- Java-style Display tab: Sneak, Living, On Fire, Look at, Flip, Rotation X/Y/Z, Scale, gamerules
- Fixed: NPCs, outlines and capes vanishing at some camera angles

**Updating from V2:** delete the old NPC Studio packs first, then import V3. Existing worlds keep working; for the new panel make a new world or re-add the packs to your world.
